# Koi MVP Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Build Koi's MVP: a place-first, privacy-first dating app for Bangkok (Thai and English) where two people who were introduced privately hook pinkies, unlock into a plan at a listed public venue, chat briefly, and close without ghosting.

**Architecture:** Next.js (App Router) PWA over Postgres. All rules that touch the other person live in the unchanged `src/rules/pinky-core.ts` plus a small `src/rules/koi-rules.ts`. Thin services take `ctx: Ctx` (db, clock) and run each state change in one transaction. Route handlers are thin and return identical bodies where the spec requires it.

**Tech Stack:** Node 22, TypeScript, Next.js, Postgres 16 (docker-compose), Kysely + pg, zod, sharp, next-intl, MapLibre GL + OpenStreetMap tiles, Vitest (real Postgres), Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-25-koi-mvp-design.md` (approved MVP + post-MVP section). Rules: `pinky-template-2026-11-25/PINKY-TEMPLATE.md`, `pinky-core.ts`, `schema.sql`. Brief: `~/jurgun/exports/koi-app-prompt.md` (read only).

## Global Constraints

- Time zone Asia/Bangkok, fixed offset UTC+7 (`PLACE_SETTINGS.localOffsetMin = 420`). Server TZ must not matter.
- Age 18+ from birth date. Photos 1–6. Exactly 3 prompts, each at most 150 characters (count graphemes, not code units). Interests at most 6.
- Pins: at least 3, at most 5, listed public venues only. Map counts: rounded down to 5, hidden below 5, refreshed daily, never "here now".
- Open hours: weekly grid, Morning 09–12, Afternoon 13–17, Evening 18–22. Proposed time: at least 24 h ahead, within 14 days, shared window at least 2 h.
- Open cap: open or paused Pinkies + outstanding taps < 3.
- Chat: text and emoji only, 6 messages per person until the plan has a place and a time, "A few more questions" once per Pinky adds 6 each.
- Note at most 280 characters. Place name at most 80.
- Plan notices: at most one unread, held during quiet hours 22:00–08:00. Close: ask at 10:00 the next morning, 48 h window.
- End kindly presets (exact text): "Thank you for the date. I hope you find the right person." / "I'd rather we were friends." / "I'm not ready right now. I'm sorry."
- Notification text and email subjects must never match `/\b(date|dating|match|koi|pinky|romance)\b/i`, in Thai or English.
- No swiping, feeds, streaks, counters, countdowns, boosts, paid visibility, ratings or "people nearby". Rose `#C42E57` appears only at the unlock moment and on primary actions.
- Colour tokens (verbatim): night `#14201B`, paper `#F4F6F1`, paper-2 `#E8EDE6`, card `#FBFCF9`, ink `#15201C`, body `#46534D`, line `#CCD6CF`, rose `#C42E57` (`#F28CA6` on dark), leaf `#2E7D5B`, leaf-soft `#DCEFE4`. Every screen works at 375 px, in Thai and English, light and dark. Tap targets at least 44 px. WCAG 2.2 AA.
- `src/rules/pinky-core.ts` is copied verbatim from the template and never edited.

## Modern-dating hardening (NEW, not in the approved MVP: needs your OK)

Principle: organic means a safeguard shows up as part of the product (a plan, a gentle line, a quiet default), never a wall, a score or a scolding. Growth is organic too: no seeded fake people, no boosts, intros open in an area only when enough real people are there.

| Flaw in modern dating | Fix | Task |
|---|---|---|
| Fake profiles, stolen photos | Short selfie check before you can hook a pinky (viewer-side rule), a plain "Verified" tick, provider behind an adapter | 5 |
| Stranger's GPS in photo files | Photos re-encoded, EXIF/GPS stripped | 5 |
| Matches nobody can reach (one-sided exposure) | Introductions are symmetric and one-time per pair | 8 |
| Leaking each other's filters through who you're shown | Your own filters only hide cards on your side; the other person's age range and openness are checked silently at unlock (`pinky-core.ts` has no age check, so `koi-rules.ts` adds it) | 1, 9 |
| Bots, mass tapping, code guessing | Per-account and per-IP rate limits, hashed one-time codes, no account enumeration | 3, 9 |
| Romance scams, links, money asks, moving off-app on day one | Links never sent. Contact details and money talk get a gentle note to the sender until both confirmed the plan. Message text never logged | 1, 11 |
| Unsafe or private meeting spots | Plan place must be a listed public venue, time must be future and within 30 days, safe-venue code phrase and a safety line inside the space, "Share your plan" built on the person's own device | 10 |
| Pen-pal matches that never meet | Pinky with no place and time after 5 days closes quietly for both, nobody blamed | 13 |
| Pestering after a no | Ended pair never re-introduced, never re-tappable. Blocking is silent and a blocked person just stops seeing you | 8, 13 |
| Under-18s | Birth-date gate, a "may be under 18" report reason hides the profile at once pending review (honest limit: age is self-declared) | 3, 13 |
| Sensitive data (gender, orientation) under Thai PDPA | Explicit consent before any dating data, withdrawing it deletes dating data, full account deletion, own-data export | 4, 13 |
| Engagement hooks | No streaks or counters, a fixed daily intro cap, quiet-hours notices, "that's today's set" empty state | 8, 15 |
| Thin pool at launch feels dead | An area opens intros at 30 verified people (my call), until then honest "we'll tell you when intros start" | 8 |

## Review Focus

1. **Thin pool.** A district with no or few people: empty intros, no filler or fake cards, honest copy (Task 8).
2. **Thai text length.** A 150-grapheme Thai prompt with combining marks is accepted, 151 rejected (Task 4).
3. **Bangkok midnight.** Intro day, quiet hours and the 10:00 close ask are right with the server in UTC and a plan at 23:30 Bangkok (Tasks 7, 14, 15).
4. **Simultaneous taps.** Two people tap in the same instant: exactly one Pinky, one notice each (Task 9).
5. **Counterpart vanishes.** Tap, edit or message after the other person deleted their account, turned dating off, or blocked: same response shape, no crash, outsider 404 (Tasks 9, 10, 13).

## Decisions to check (calls I made)

1. **Introductions are symmetric and one-time per pair.** A one-sided "daily set" would almost never let both people see each other, so no pair could ever tap each other. This refines the approved Browse decision and still uses area only.
2. **Tap window 7 days after introduction, 10 intros a day, 5 days to a plan, 30 people to open an area.** All are settings, easy to change.
3. **Plan place must be a listed venue.** The template allows free text up to 80 characters, which would let someone suggest a private address.
4. **Age ranges are checked silently at unlock** in `koi-rules.ts`, because `pinky-core.ts` does not check age and I won't edit it.
5. **Hardening is new scope** (the table above). Task 5's selfie check needs a real vendor before launch; the dev provider is blocked in production.
