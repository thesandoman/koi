# Surface brief: Koi marketing site (index)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: a single adult in Bangkok arriving on a phone, often from a friend's link. Action: play the demo, then join the area waitlist (one email, one area, explicit consent). Proof: the working demo itself; no statistics, testimonials or partner names exist. Constraints: static, Thai and English parity, brand tokens and logo pinned by the owner, no cookies or third-party requests. Owner's rejects: generic SaaS template, typical dating-app look, cold or clinical, too busy. Owner pinned the framing: "use modern UI framing. 21stdev" (tilting phone hero and spring segmented control adapted from 21st.dev).
Unresolved: waitlist storage provider; verification vendor; Thai native review.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a phone you can actually use. Koi's whole idea (place first, private mutual tap, a plan, a kind close) is demonstrated by one device that follows you down the page, instead of described by feature cards. Refuses the category default of hero image plus three feature cards plus pricing.

OWN-WORLD: A tracing-paper city map. Cool paper ground (`#F4F6F1`) or deep pond green-black (`#14201B`) by system setting; hairline map ink, dotted roads, a river drawn as a wide soft band, leaf-green pins; typography in Bricolage Grotesque set large and tight, Inter for reading, Noto Sans Thai for Thai. Rose is held back entirely for the unlock moment and the primary actions. Signature: a ripple, like a koi surfacing, radiates from every pin you drop and, in rose, from the unlock. Recognizable with the words removed: a phone frame, a pin-dotted river map, concentric ripple rings.

STORY: The visitor understands in seconds that Koi is about places, not faces; plays it (pin three places, hook a pinky, see Sam's phone show nothing until both hook, see one place and one time appear, see that "no" and "no answer" look the same); believes it because they watched it work and the page says plainly what Koi never does; then joins their area's list, knowing Koi opens one area at a time and is not launched yet.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Centered headline "Pick the place first." at about 6rem on desktop (3.25rem on a phone), a one-sentence lede and one live hint line beneath it; a solid-paper nav (wordmark left, language control and a rose "Join the list" right). Below the headline a phone, tilted back about 24 degrees, rises from the lower edge, its screen already showing the pin-dotted Bangkok map with tappable pins; as you scroll the phone flattens and climbs over the headline. The primary action is the rose "Join the list" in the nav; the first interaction is tapping a pin.

FORM: Tilting-phone hero (21st.dev "Phone Scroll Hero") leading into a sticky-phone scroll story with a spring segmented control to switch between your phone and Sam's. seed: none (pinned), pinned by the owner's words "use modern UI framing. 21stdev" and ratified after the build: the owner answered "Ratify this direction" to the question that named the pond-map world, the koi ripples and the following phone.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
